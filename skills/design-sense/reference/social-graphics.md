# Social media graphics

## Canvas sizes (px)
| Use | Size | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / Threads post | 1080×1350 (portrait, best reach) or 1080×1080 | 4:5 / 1:1 |
| Stories / Reels / Shorts cover | 1080×1920 | 9:16 |
| Facebook / LinkedIn link image (OG) | 1200×630 | 1.91:1 |
| X (Twitter) in-feed image | 1600×900 | 16:9 |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280×720 | 16:9 |
| LINE / blog header | 1200×628 or 1040×1040 | — |

## Safe areas
- Stories/Reels: keep text out of the top ~250px and bottom ~340px (UI overlays) and 60px from the sides.
- Square/portrait posts: 60–80px margin; profile previews may crop to the center 1:1.
- OG images: important text within the center 1000×520.

## Readability
- Assume it is seen at ~360px wide on a phone: headline ≥ 64px on a 1080 canvas (≈ 6% of width), body ≥ 36px, max ~12 zh-TW characters or ~7 English words per line, ≤ 3 lines of headline.
- Contrast ≥ 4.5:1; on photos add a solid band or a 40–60% scrim, not a thin text shadow.
- One message, one focal point, brand mark small in a consistent corner.
- zh-TW: use bold sans (Noto Sans TC Black/Bold, 思源黑體) for headlines, no italic, no faux-bold; full-width punctuation; avoid orphaned single characters on the last line.
- Export PNG for text/graphics, JPEG/WebP quality 85 for photos; sRGB; keep files < 1 MB where possible.
- Redact personal data (faces, plates, emails, phone numbers) before posting.
